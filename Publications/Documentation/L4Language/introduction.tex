
\begin{tcolorbox}[breakable,colback=red!5!white,colframe=red!75!black,
  title=Some words of cautioning]

  This document has been started at the end of the stay of Martin Strecker
  (MS) at CCLAW in 2021, slightly modified again in 2022 and then remained
  unchanged until a half-hearted overhaul in mid-2024. Color boxes of like
  this one will comment on the status of the pieces of text this document is
  composed of.

  This document describes a version of the L4 language that has internally
  been dubbed \textsc{BabyL4} and that has later been superseded by
  \textsc{NaturalL4}, which is now the official language version. 

  There are also yellow color boxes that give my (MS) personal opinion on
  certain topics.

\end{tcolorbox}
